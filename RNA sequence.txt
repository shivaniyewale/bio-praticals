
import pandas as pd

# Read Dataset
data = pd.read_csv("rna_seq.csv")

print("RNA-Seq Dataset\n")
print(data)

# Calculate Fold Change
data["Fold Change"] = data["Treated"] / data["Control"]

status = []

for fc in data["Fold Change"]:
    if fc >= 2:
        status.append("Upregulated")
    elif fc <= 0.5:
        status.append("Downregulated")
    else:
        status.append("No Significant Change")

data["Status"] = status

print("\nDifferential Gene Expression\n")
print(data)

4. What is fold change? Explain how it is used to identify upregulated and downregulated genes.
Fold Change (FC) is the ratio of gene expression in the treated sample to the control sample.

Formula:
\[
FC = \frac{Treated\ Expression}{Control\ Expression}
\]

Classification:
- FC ≥ 2: Upregulated gene.
- FC ≤ 0.5: Downregulated gene.
- 0.5 less than FC less than 2: No significant change according to the given threshold.
Example:

If Control = 50 and Treated = 100:
\[
FC = \frac{100}{50}=2
\]

Therefore, the gene is upregulated.

what is upregulated and downregulated













In simple words:
1. Upregulated gene
Upregulated means the gene produces more RNA (shows increased gene expression) in the treated sample compared to the control sample.

Example:
- Control = 50
- Treated = 120
\[
FC = \frac{120}{50}=2.4
\]

Since FC ≥ 2, the gene is Upregulated.
2. Downregulated gene
Downregulated means the gene produces less RNA (shows decreased gene expression) in the treated sample compared to the control sample.

Example:
- Control = 200
- Treated = 100
\[
FC = \frac{100}{200}=0.5
\]

Since FC ≤ 0.5, the gene is Downregulated.
3. Difference between them
Upregulated	Downregulated
Gene expression increases.	Gene expression decreases.
More RNA is produced.	Less RNA is produced.
Fold Change ≥ 2 (our practical).	Fold Change ≤ 0.5 (our practical).

Easy trick to remember:
- Up = Expression goes UP ⬆️
- Down = Expression goes DOWN ⬇️
Viva answer: Upregulation is an increase in gene expression, whereas downregulation is a decrease in gene expression.

Suppose a patient has an infection and is given medicine.

After taking medicine, a gene responsible for immune response becomes more active.

Fold Change = 150 / 50 = 3

More gene expression = Upregulated.




2. Downregulated gene ⬇️

After treatment, a gene responsible for inflammation becomes less active.

Example: Gene expression decreases from 200 to 100.

Fold Change = 100 / 200 = 0.5

Less gene expression = Downregulated.
