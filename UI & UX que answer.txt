Practical 1: Design User Persona
Q1. What is a User Persona?

A User Persona is a fictional representation of a target user based on their needs, goals, behaviour and problems.

Q2. Why do we create a User Persona?

To understand users and design a product according to their requirements.

Q3. What is a target user?

A target user is a person or group of people for whom we design a product.

Q4. What is a fictional user?

A fictional user is an imaginary person created to represent the characteristics of real users.

Q5. What are the main elements of a User Persona?
- Name and age
- Occupation
- Goals
- Needs
- Pain points
- Behaviour
- Motivation
Q6. What are pain points?

Pain points are the problems or difficulties faced by users while using a product.

Q7. What are user goals?

User goals are the things users want to achieve using a product.

Q8. What is user behaviour?

User behaviour describes how users interact with a product or application.

Q9. How does a User Persona improve UI/UX design?

It helps designers create a user-friendly interface according to user needs.

Q10. Which tools are used to create a User Persona?

Figma, Canva and Google Forms.

Q11. Is a User Persona a real person?

No. It is a fictional representation of a group of target users.

Q12. What is user research?

User research is the process of collecting information about users, their needs and expectations.

Practical 2: Online Learning Platform – Wireframe Design
Q13. What is a wireframe?

A wireframe is a basic blueprint of a website or application that shows the layout and arrangement of elements.

Q14. What is the purpose of a wireframe?

To plan the structure and layout of an application before designing the final interface.

Q15. What is a low-fidelity wireframe?

A simple design that uses basic shapes, lines and text without detailed colours or graphics.

Q16. What is a high-fidelity wireframe?

A detailed representation of an interface that looks closer to the final design.

Q17. Which tool is used to create wireframes?

Figma, Adobe XD, Balsamiq and Sketch.

Q18. What are the main features of an Online Learning Platform?
- Course listings
- Video lectures
- Quizzes
- Progress tracking
Q19. What is course listing?

Course listing displays the available courses from which users can select.

Q20. What is progress tracking?

Progress tracking monitors the completion of lessons, courses and activities.

Q21. Why are quizzes used in an online learning platform?

To test students' knowledge and understanding.

Q22. What is navigation in UI design?

Navigation helps users move from one screen or section to another.

Q23. What is a dashboard?

A dashboard is a screen that displays important information in one place.

Q24. How can we improve an Online Learning Platform design?

By adding simple navigation, search filters, progress bars, notifications and clear buttons.

Practical 3: Social Fitness App – Wireframes and Prototype
Q25. What is a Social Fitness App?

A Social Fitness App helps users track workouts, set fitness goals, connect with friends and share progress.

Q26. What is workout tracking?

Workout tracking means recording physical activities, exercise duration, calories and other fitness information.

Q27. What is goal setting?

Goal setting allows users to define fitness targets such as daily steps or workout time.

Q28. What is a fitness dashboard?

It displays daily fitness information such as steps, calories burned and workout progress.

Q29. What is a social feed?

A social feed displays posts, activities and updates shared by users and their friends.

Q30. What are social features?

Social features allow users to connect, communicate, like posts and share activities.

Q31. What is a prototype?

A prototype is an interactive model of an application that demonstrates how the interface works.

Q32. What is the difference between a wireframe and a prototype?

A wireframe shows the basic layout, while a prototype demonstrates screen interactions and navigation.

Q33. What is Figma Prototype mode?

It is a feature in Figma used to connect screens and create interactive designs.

Q34. What is Auto Layout in Figma?

Auto Layout automatically arranges and spaces elements inside a frame or component.

Q35. What is a progress bar?

A progress bar visually represents the completion of a task or goal.

Q36. How can we improve a Social Fitness App?

By adding workout reminders, achievement badges, progress charts, friend requests and privacy settings.

Practical 4: Product Packaging Mockup
Q37. What is a mockup?

A mockup is a realistic visual representation of a design showing how the final product will look.

Q38. What is product packaging?

Product packaging is the material and design used to protect, store and present a product.

Q39. What is a Product Packaging Mockup?

It is a realistic representation of product packaging before actual manufacturing.

Q40. Why is product packaging important?
- Protects the product.
- Attracts customers.
- Provides product information.
- Builds brand identity.
Q41. What are the main elements of packaging design?

Logo, product name, colours, images, ingredients, weight and product information.

Q42. What are the different sides of a packaging box?

Front, back, left, right, top and bottom.

Q43. What information is displayed on the front side?

Brand name, product name, logo and product image.

Q44. What information is displayed on the back side?

Ingredients, usage instructions, manufacturer details and other product information.

Q45. What is a dieline?

A dieline is a flat template showing where packaging should be cut, folded and assembled.

Q46. What is a 3D packaging mockup?

It is a three-dimensional representation of packaging that shows different sides and angles.

Q47. What is brand identity?

Brand identity is the visual representation of a brand through its logo, colours, fonts and design.

Q48. Which tools are used for packaging mockups?

Pacdora, Canva, Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator.

Q49. Why are colours important in packaging?

Colours attract customers and communicate the product's identity.

Practical 5: Poster or Flyer Mockup
Q50. What is a poster?

A poster is a visual design used to promote an event, product, service or campaign.

Q51. What is a flyer?

A flyer is a small printed or digital promotional document containing important information.

Q52. What is the difference between a poster and a flyer?
Poster	Flyer
Usually displayed on walls.	Usually distributed to people.
Attracts attention from a distance.	Provides promotional details.
Often larger in size.	Usually smaller in size.

Q53. What are the main elements of a poster?

Heading, images, colours, event details, date, time, venue and contact information.

Q54. What is the purpose of a poster?

To attract attention and communicate information effectively.

Q55. What are common poster sizes?

A4, A3 and A2.

Q56. What is a digital poster?

A poster designed for digital platforms such as websites and social media.

Q57. What is visual hierarchy?

Visual hierarchy is arranging design elements according to their importance so users notice the most important information first.

Q58. What is alignment?

Alignment means arranging design elements properly to maintain a clean layout.

Q59. Why should we use attractive fonts?

To make the poster readable and visually appealing.

Q60. How can we improve poster design?

By using attractive colours, clear headings, readable fonts, relevant images and proper spacing.

Q61. Where can we display a poster mockup?

On college walls, notice boards, display stands, billboards and digital screens.

Practical 6: Recipe Finder Application Using Figma
Q62. What is a Recipe Finder Application?

A Recipe Finder Application helps users find recipes based on ingredients, categories and dietary preferences.

Q63. What is the purpose of a Recipe Finder App?

To help users discover recipes and prepare food easily.

Q64. What are the main features of a Recipe Finder App?
- Recipe search
- Food categories
- Dietary filters
- Recipe details
- Cooking instructions
- Favourite recipes
Q65. What is ingredient-based searching?

It allows users to find recipes using the ingredients available to them.

Q66. What are dietary restrictions?

Dietary restrictions are food limitations such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free diets.

Q67. What is a search filter?

A search filter helps users find results according to selected conditions.

Q68. What are recipe details?

Recipe details include ingredients, cooking time, difficulty level, serving size and recipe information.

Q69. What are cooking instructions?

Cooking instructions are step-by-step directions explaining how to prepare a dish.

Q70. What is the favourite feature?

It allows users to save recipes for future use.

Q71. What is a bottom navigation bar?

It is a menu displayed at the bottom of a screen to navigate between different sections.

Q72. Why do we use food images in a Recipe Finder App?

To make recipes attractive and help users understand the dishes.

Q73. How can we improve a Recipe Finder App?

By adding ingredient search, dietary filters, cooking timers, ratings, food images and voice search.
